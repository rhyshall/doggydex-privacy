1. About This Policy

DoggyDex is a dog breed quiz and collection game operated by Rhys Hall and intended for players of all ages, including children under 13. This policy explains how we handle information when you use the app, its web version, account features, support services, and this public privacy-policy webpage. Features and data collection can differ by platform.

2. Information We Collect

Account and profile information

We process your email address, account identifier, trainer name, sign-in provider, and account verification information. If you sign in with Google, we receive information provided through that sign-in, such as your name, email address, and profile picture. Email and password sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication.

You can choose a custom profile photo. The selected, edited image is saved with your profile in Cloud Firestore. Removing a custom photo may restore your Google profile picture if one is available. Choosing a photo does not authorize us to upload your entire photo library.

Gameplay information

We store quiz answers and results, scores, streaks, XP, levels, discoveries, unlocked coats, trainer choices, milestones, and related progress records. These records support gameplay, synchronization, recovery of interrupted saves, and leaderboards.

Technical, advertising, and analytics information

Our service providers process technical information needed to deliver and protect their services. This can include IP addresses, device or browser information, identifiers, requests, and diagnostic logs. Android advertising and web analytics are described separately below.

Support information

If you contact us, we receive your email address, message, and any information or attachments you choose to provide. Please do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.

3. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Create, verify, secure, and manage accounts.
  • Run quizzes, calculate rewards, save progress, and synchronize devices.
  • Display trainer profiles, collections, and leaderboards.
  • Remember preferences and recover pending saves.
  • Display advertisements and manage advertising privacy choices.
  • Understand use of the web version and diagnose service problems.
  • Prevent abuse, respond to support requests, and process account deletion or other privacy requests.
  • Meet applicable legal requirements.

4. Public Trainer Information

Other signed-in players can view trainer profiles and collections through DoggyDex's social and leaderboard features. Visible information can include your trainer name, profile photo, trainer style and path, starter selection, XP or level, quiz statistics, rankings, milestones, discovered breeds, and unlocked coats.

Profile information can also include account creation and discovery or milestone dates. Older profile photos stored in Firebase Storage may be accessible without signing in to anyone who has their image link. Google profile pictures are loaded from Google-provided image URLs.

Choose your trainer name and photo with this visibility in mind. Your email address and authentication credentials are not included in the public trainer information returned by these features.

5. Google Services and Data Sharing

DoggyDex uses Firebase Authentication for accounts; Cloud Firestore and Cloud Functions for game records and server operations; Google Sign-In for optional Google authentication; Firebase Storage for hosted images, including support for older profile-photo uploads; and Firebase Hosting for hosted account-action pages. Android builds use Google AdMob and its User Messaging Platform for advertisements and advertising consent. Supported web versions initialize Google Analytics through Firebase.

We share information with these services as needed for the features described in this policy. We may also disclose information where required by law or reasonably necessary to investigate abuse and protect users or service security. Information that you make visible through trainer and leaderboard features is shared with other players as described above.

Google processes information under the terms and privacy practices applicable to each service. Information may be processed outside Canada, including in the United States, where privacy laws may differ.

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Firebase Privacy and Security: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google Advertising Technologies: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

6. Android Advertisements and Consent

DoggyDex can show occasional full-screen interstitial advertisements at transitions after completed quizzes. Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share IP addresses, approximate location inferred from IP addresses, advertising and other device identifiers, app and ad interactions, and diagnostic information. Google uses this information for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention.

The app does not deliberately attach your trainer name, email address, quiz answers, or progression records to AdMob ad requests. The SDK's own collection described above still applies.

Before requesting ads, DoggyDex uses Google's User Messaging Platform to check whether ads may be requested and to present a consent message where required. If this check fails or ads are not permitted, gameplay remains available without advertisements. Your choices and applicable regional requirements affect which kinds of ads Google may serve. Declining personalized advertising does not necessarily remove all ads or all associated data processing.

Advertising choices presented in Google's consent messages govern future advertising requests; changing them does not itself delete information already processed. The current app does not offer a visible control to reopen Google's advertising consent form after it has been dismissed. To ask about changing or withdrawing advertising consent, contact us using the address below. Android's system settings provide advertising identifier controls, where supported; these controls do not replace choices in a consent form. For questions or requests about advertising privacy, contact doggydex.support@gmail.com.

Test builds use Google's test advertisement inventory. Test ads do not mean that the SDK makes no network requests or processes no technical information.

7. Web Analytics and Local Storage

The supported web version uses Google Analytics through Firebase to understand usage. Analytics may use cookies or similar browser storage and process browser/device information, identifiers, page visits, and interaction events. Android advertising consent does not control web analytics. Your browser provides cookie and site-storage controls; blocking or clearing storage may affect sign-in and saved preferences. The current Android app does not initialize the Firebase web Analytics SDK; AdMob can still process advertising analytics as described above.

DoggyDex also stores information on your device for sign-in, preferences, cached game data, and pending progress synchronization. Advertising frequency records include completed-quiz counts, recent run identifiers, and the last ad time. These frequency records are device-specific and separate from your game account.

Clearing app or browser data removes local information but does not delete your server-side account. It can also remove progress that has not yet synchronized.

8. Retention and Account Deletion

We retain account and gameplay records while needed to provide your account and the features described here.

To delete your account in the app, open Account, choose Delete Account, and complete the identity verification and confirmation steps. Deletion removes your Firebase sign-in account, profile and custom photo, server-side gameplay and collection records, username reservation, and associated leaderboard records handled by DoggyDex's deletion process. Deleting DoggyDex does not delete your Google account.

Deletion is processed in the background and may continue after you leave the app. An accepted request is not the same as completed deletion; the app displays deletion status, and interrupted server cleanup is retried. Contact us if deletion remains pending or you need help.

You can also request deletion without using the app by emailing doggydex.support@gmail.com with the subject DoggyDex Account Deletion Request. We may need to verify account ownership before acting. Do not email your password.

After server deletion completes, a minimal record containing the account identifier, deletion status, and processing timestamps is temporarily retained to prevent stale access from recreating data. Scheduled cleanup removes this record after at least 24 hours. Service interruptions can extend that period.

Current custom profile photos stored in Firestore are included in account deletion. Removal of older photo files in Firebase Storage depends on the storage buckets configured for server cleanup. Contact support if an older image link remains accessible after deletion.

Local account caches are cleared through the app's deletion and sign-out handling. We cannot remotely erase cached copies on devices that never reconnect. Device-wide preferences or ad-frequency records may remain until app data is cleared. Other users may have retained screenshots or other copies of information previously visible to them.

Service logs, backups, administrative exports, and support correspondence can have separate retention and cleanup requirements. Account deletion does not automatically erase information independently retained by Google for advertising or analytics. Contact us about related data or privacy requests so we can review the applicable records and provider processes. Information may be retained where required by law or necessary to resolve a dispute or protect service security.

9. Security

We use access controls, authenticated account operations, and encrypted network connections provided by our service infrastructure to help protect information. No system or transmission method is completely secure. Keep your account credentials private and contact us if you suspect unauthorized access.

10. Children's Privacy

DoggyDex is intended for all ages, including children under 13. The account, gameplay, profile, and technical information described in this policy can relate to children as well as adults.

Parents and guardians should review the information collected and the visibility of trainer profiles before allowing a child to create an account or share a photo. Trainer names, photos, and collections can be visible to other signed-in players. Use a nickname and avoid including a child's full name, school, address, contact details, or other sensitive information in public-facing features.

A parent or guardian can contact doggydex.support@gmail.com to request a review or deletion of a child's information, ask about its use or disclosure, or request that further collection or use stop. We may need to verify the requester's authority and identify the relevant account before acting. Account deletion is described in section 8; some account-based features require information to operate and may no longer be available if that information is removed or collection is declined.

An advertising consent message or a parent's supervision is not a substitute for verifiable parental consent where the law requires it. This policy describes the intended audience and current data practices; it does not itself obtain parental consent.

11. Your Privacy Choices and Rights

You can edit supported profile details in Account, remove or change your custom photo, and request account deletion.

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may also have rights to access or correct information, obtain a copy, request deletion, object to or restrict certain processing, withdraw consent, or complain to a privacy regulator. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal. These rights can be subject to exceptions and identity verification.

Send requests or questions to doggydex.support@gmail.com. We will review them and respond in accordance with applicable law.

12. Policy Updates and Contact

We may update this policy as DoggyDex or its data practices change. We will update the effective date and provide additional notice or request consent where required.
